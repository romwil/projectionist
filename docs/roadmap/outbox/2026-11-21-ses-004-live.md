# Handoff: realtime session channel

**Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Server item:** SES-004 · **Capability:** `shared_sessions` 1

Depends on `session_object` 1.

## What changed

A WebSocket at `/api/sessions/{id}/live` sends the current state on connect. The client sends commands:

`play`, `pause`, `seek` (`ms`), `skip` (`s`), `rate` (`1` or `2`), `captions` (`id` or `off`), `level`, `end`.

The starter may send all of them. The member signed in on the screen may send transport (`play`, `pause`, `seek`, `skip`). Anyone else is refused. The same body is accepted at `POST /api/sessions/{id}/commands`.

Every accepted event includes `server_time_ms`, `position_ms`, `state`, and `level`. While `state` is `playing`, the playhead is `position_ms` plus the time since `server_time_ms`. While paused, buffering, or ended, the playhead stays at `position_ms`.

## What the client does

- Connect when `shared_sessions` is at least 1, with the same credential used for the session.
- Apply commands through the socket or the post route. Do not change the level from the screen.
- Estimate the playhead from the last event. Do not poll the session object for every frame.

## Done when (client)

- The starter can pause, and the screen sees the paused state.
- A member who is not in the session cannot open the channel.
