# Handoff: handheld captions

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Server item:** SES-006 · **Capability:** `session_captions` 1

Depends on `shared_sessions` 1.

## What changed

`GET /api/sessions/{id}/captions?language=en` returns timed cues from the household subtitle cache for a member who is in the session. The response is the language and the cues. It does not change the room's captions.

A member who has not joined gets 404, and the line is not in that response.

## What the client does

- Read the feed when `session_captions` is at least 1.
- Draw cues against the playhead from the live channel.
- Keep the language on the device.

## Done when (client)

- A joined device shows the line. A device outside the session does not.
