# Handoff: moving a session to another screen

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Server item:** SES-007 · **Capability:** `session_handoff` 1

Depends on `shared_sessions` 1.

## What changed

`POST /api/sessions/{id}/handoff` with `screen_id` keeps the session id, the position, and the level, and points the session at the new screen. The member signed in on the old screen can no longer read it. Only the starter can move it.

## What the client does

- Offer handoff when `session_handoff` is at least 1.
- The screen that was playing stops. The new screen reads the same session.

## Done when (client)

- After a move, the new screen is at the same position and the old screen is out.
