# Platform handoff: Doctor summary and a member diagnostic report

**Date:** 2026-11-26 · **Server items:** PRD-026, PRD-027, PRD-028 · **Depends on:** SEC-035, SEC-036 · **Client item waiting:** APL-067

## What the platform will offer

*Proposed* capability `doctor` is `1` when the check registry is available.

An owner can read a summary: each check is green or needs attention, with a plain explanation and a fix. A member can post a diagnostic report to the owner's inbox. The report may include the device model, the app version, which address answered, TLS result, credential status, and error codes. It must not include titles or play history.

An owner device can post a reachability result for `away` and `app_away` (whether it answered, and whether TLS matched). Doctor stores that result.

## When

This is the community-launch track. It starts after container hardening and the two doors. It is not part of the current front-door or playback work. The client waits until `doctor` is on a released server.

## Privacy

Reports stay on the household server. They are not a telemetry upload.
